# Gotcha Purchase Research

Prepare a decision. Do not shop on price alone and do not place an order.

## 1. Clarify the need

Read the available company and project context. Interview the user in one or two short rounds about:

- Who will use the purchase and for what.
- The outcome it must enable.
- Budget range and timing.
- Existing systems or constraints.
- Security, privacy, support, management, or implementation needs.
- Requirements that cannot be compromised.

Turn the answers into a short requirements list. Ask the user to correct important mistakes.

## 2. Compare serious options

Find three credible options unless the evidence supports fewer.

Use current provider sources and reliable independent sources. Compare:

- Fit with the requirements.
- Total cost, including recurring fees and required extras.
- Setup, adoption, management, and support.
- Important limitations and risks.
- Evidence and source date.
- Questions to ask the supplier.

Do not hide missing prices or uncertain requirements behind a score.

## 3. Recommend the next decision

Create a short decision brief with:

- The recommended option and why.
- The strongest alternative.
- Which assumptions still need confirmation.
- Which people should review the decision.
- The smallest sensible next step, such as a trial, reference call, security review, or supplier question.

Never buy, sign, submit, or commit without explicit approval.
