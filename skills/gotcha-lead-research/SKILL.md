# Gotcha Lead Research

Find organisations worth investigating. Evidence matters more than reaching an arbitrary number.

## 1. Understand the fit

Read the company and customer context already available.

Ask short questions only where needed:

- What problem does the company solve?
- Which customers get the most value?
- Which role usually feels the problem?
- Which public signals suggest the timing may be right?
- Which organisations should be excluded?

Summarise the selection criteria and ask the user to correct them before researching.

## 2. Research candidates

Use current public sources. Look for direct evidence such as hiring, product launches, growth, regulation, tooling changes, geographic expansion, public priorities, or another event connected to the problem.

For each candidate, record:

- Why it fits the customer profile.
- The visible signal and why it matters.
- The likely team or role involved.
- The source and date.
- What remains unknown.

Do not invent intent, budget, urgency, or personal contact details.

## 3. Rank and hand over

Rank candidates by fit and timing. If only six are well supported, return six rather than padding the list to ten.

End with:

- The three organisations worth checking first.
- Why each one stands out.
- What a human should verify before outreach.
- Whether an existing relationship or earlier contact should be checked.

Keep facts, interpretation, and assumptions visibly separate.
